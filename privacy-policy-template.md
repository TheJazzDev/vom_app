## Introduction

CSMC - Valley of Mercy ("we," "our," or "us") operates the CSMC - Valley of Mercy mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our App.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access the App.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information
We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you register on the App, including but not limited to:

- **Name:** First name and last name
- **Email Address:** For account creation and communication
- **Phone Number:** For account verification and contact
- **Membership Information:** Church membership details, department, band affiliation
- **Profile Picture:** Optional user-uploaded photo
- **Date of Birth:** For birthday notifications and age-appropriate content

### Usage Data
We may automatically collect certain information about your device and how you interact with our App, including:

- **Device Information:** Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers
- **Log Data:** App usage statistics, crash reports, and performance data
- **Location Data:** [Only if your app uses location - if not, remove this section]

### Information from Firebase
Our App uses Firebase services (Firebase Authentication, Firestore Database) which may collect:

- Authentication tokens
- User session data
- Database interaction logs

## How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

1. **Account Management**
   - Create and maintain your user account
   - Authenticate your identity
   - Provide personalized app experience

2. **Church Communication**
   - Send announcements and updates
   - Notify you of upcoming events and programmes
   - Share prayer requests and testimonies
   - Birthday notifications

3. **Community Features**
   - Display member directory information
   - Enable connections between church members
   - Facilitate ministry and department participation

4. **App Improvement**
   - Analyze app usage and performance
   - Fix bugs and improve functionality
   - Develop new features

5. **Legal Compliance**
   - Comply with legal obligations
   - Enforce our terms and conditions
   - Protect our rights and the rights of other users

- **Testimonies:** Testimonies you submit may be shared with the church community
